# Oil_Price_Crisis_Data_Analysis_2026
Analyzed global oil price fluctuations and geopolitical crisis impacts using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI. Built interactive dashboards to study Brent &amp; WTI crude trends, inflation impact, market volatility, and India’s economic dependency on global oil supply disruptions.


-- Global Oil Crisis Data Analysis
--Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of global geopolitical crises on crude oil prices and their economic effects, especially on India. Using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI, the project transforms raw oil market data into meaningful insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and interactive dashboards.

The analysis focuses on Brent Crude and WTI Crude oil price fluctuations, market volatility, inflation impact, and global supply disruptions caused by geopolitical tensions.

--Objectives
Analyze historical crude oil price trends
Compare Brent Crude and WTI Crude prices
Study the impact of geopolitical conflicts on oil markets
Understand the economic impact on India
Create an interactive Power BI dashboard for business insights
Perform SQL-based exploratory data analysis
